# CampusEats System Brief

## What the System Does
CampusEats is an on-campus food ordering and delivery platform designed specifically for IIIT VADODARA students, faculty, and staff. It seamlessly connects the campus community with on-campus dining halls, local cafeterias, and nearby registered restaurants. The system allows users to browse live menus, customize their food orders, process digital payments, and track their deliveries in real-time, providing a convenient dining experience within the geographical boundaries of the college campus.

## Who Uses It
* **Customers (Students & Staff):** People who use the platform to browse food options and place orders.
* **Vendors (Restaurants & Cafeterias):** Food service providers who receive orders, prepare the food, and update the menu.
* **Delivery Agents:** Typically students who sign up to deliver food items across campus to earn extra income.
* **System Administrators:** Staff who manage user accounts, oversee daily operations, resolve disputes, and maintain platform security.

## Nouns (The Things / Entities)
* User (Customer, Vendor, Delivery-Agent, Admin)
* Restaurant / Cafeteria
* Menu 
* Menu Item
* Shopping Cart
* Order
* Payment / Transaction
* Delivery Address / Location
* Review / Rating

## Verbs (The Actions / Tasks / Contracts)
* **Browse** (view restaurants and menus)
* **Add / Remove** (manage items in the shopping cart)
* **Place** (submit an order)
* **Process** (handle the payment transaction)
* **Accept / Reject** (vendor confirms or declines the order)
* **Prepare** (vendor readies the food)
* **Assign** (link a delivery runner to an order)
* **Pick up** (runner collects the food from the vendor)
* **Deliver** (runner drops off the food to the customer)
* **Track** (monitor the live status of the order)
* **Rate** (leave a review for the food or delivery service)
